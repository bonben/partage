What the paper does not say

These parameters had to be assumed. If one of them is wrong, the correction is what we need most.

  1. Automorphisms drawn from the block lower-triangular affine group (BLTA), one per SC equivalence class.
  2. AE selection by the ML-in-the-list rule (closest re-encoded candidate).
  3. Index convention of the monomial description as in the paper's text (verified on the published generator sets).
