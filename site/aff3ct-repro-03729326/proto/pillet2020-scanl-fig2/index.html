What the paper does not say

These parameters had to be assumed. If one of them is wrong, the correction is what we need most.

  1. SCAN decisions u_i = HD(lambda_0 + beta_0) on the leaves (non-systematic code, eq. (4)).
  2. Min-sum f for SCAN and BP (unscaled for BP).
  3. BP: 40 iterations without early termination, flooding schedule as in AFF3CT.

aff3ct -C POLAR -N 256 -K 128 --enc-fb-gen-method 5G -m 2.0 -M 4.5 -s 0.5 -e 300 --ter-freq 0 --dec-type SCAN --dec-implem FAST --dec-simd INTER --dec-ite 2 --enc-no-sys
