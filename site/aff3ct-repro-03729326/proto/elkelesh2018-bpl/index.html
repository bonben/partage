Not reproduced: the list gain of BPL over BP is 1.3x to 2.2x with AFF3CT against 3.5x to 4x in the paper. The decoder is bit-exact with a reference that decodes on the permuted graphs; the graph convention (assumption 3) is the main suspect.

What the paper does not say

These parameters had to be assumed. If one of them is wrong, the correction is what we need most.

  1. Design Eb/N0 of the Bhattacharyya construction: 3.5 dB, chosen because it reproduces the BP curve (the paper does not give it).
  2. Box-plus update for BP (the paper's f). The BPL curve shown uses the scaled min-sum: the box-plus BPL costs about 0.2 s per frame on one core, and its only point (6.9e-3 at 2.0 dB, 30 frame errors) is as far from the paper.
  3. Factor graph orientation and cyclic shifts: AFF3CT natural graph and its n cyclic stage shifts; the paper's "conventional" graph may be its mirror (likely cause of the BPL gap).
  4. BPL selection: ML among the candidates that satisfy the G-matrix check.
