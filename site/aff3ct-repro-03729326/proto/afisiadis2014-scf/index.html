What the paper does not say

These parameters had to be assumed. If one of them is wrong, the correction is what we need most.

  1. CRC polynomial: 0x8005 (16-IBM); the paper does not give it.
  2. Frozen set: Gaussian approximation at each simulated Eb/N0 (AFF3CT default); the paper does not give its construction.
  3. R = 1/2 counts the CRC as information for SCF (K = 512 + 16), SC is simulated without CRC (K = 512).
  4. T = 32 counts the additional attempts.
