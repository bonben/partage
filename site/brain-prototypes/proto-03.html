BRAIN : Broader Artificial Intelligence

Department of Mathematical and Electrical Engineering (MEE) · Lab-STICC UMR CNRS 6285

We study representations that help intelligent systems learn, adapt, and make sense of complex data across signals, images, and brains.

BRAIN is a research team of Lab-STICC (UMR CNRS 6285) hosted at IMT Atlantique in Brest, France, within the Mathematical and Electrical Engineering (MEE) department. We investigate fundamental and applied questions at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, Signal Processing, Computer Vision, Audio, and Computational Neuroscience.

§2Transverse Research Themes

8 domain & methodological intersections
Few-shot & transfer [T01]

Sample-efficient adaptation, cross-domain transfer, and vision-language alignment under scarce supervision.

Efficient & edge AI [T02]

Low-rank adaptation, quantization, zero-cost NAS proxies, and resource-constrained embedded inference.

Graphs & geometry [T03]

Graph signal processing, Graph Neural Networks, manifold learning, and non-Euclidean latent spaces.

Robustness [T04]

Out-of-distribution generalization, uncertainty monitoring, and reliable representation stability.

Neuroscience & EEG [T05]

Foundation models for neuroimaging, brain-computer interfaces, cortical dynamics.

Audio & music [T06]

Structural music analysis, bioacoustics, auditory representations.

Channel coding [T07]

Polar codes, error-correcting decoders, hardware transceivers.

Optimization [T08]

Combinatorial optimization, metaheuristics.
