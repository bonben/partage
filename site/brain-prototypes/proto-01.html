Broader
Artificial
Intelligence.

We study representations that help intelligent systems learn, adapt, and make sense of complex data across signals, images, and brains.

BRAIN is a research team of Lab-STICC (UMR CNRS 6285) hosted at IMT Atlantique in Brest, France, within the Mathematical and Electrical Engineering (MEE) department. We investigate fundamental and applied questions at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, Signal Processing, Computer Vision, Audio, and Computational Neuroscience.

SECTION 01 [PILLARS]

Core Research Pillars

REPRESENTATION LEARNING · 01 TO 03
01.0

Structural Axes

Our scientific agenda is structured around three complementary pillars addressing data scarcity, computational efficiency, and structural geometry in modern representation learning.

METHODOLOGY : THEORY & ALGORITHMS
01 AXIS / LOW-DATA

Thrifty AI

How to find efficient representations in the low-data regime (few-shot learning, zero-shot adaptation, vision-language models, transfer learning, foundation model adaptation).

FEW-SHOT · ZERO-SHOT · VISION-LANGUAGE · TRANSFER LEARNING · FOUNDATION MODELS
02 AXIS / EFFICIENCY

Compression of AI

How to find efficient representations to lower the computational and energy demands of AI systems (quantization, pruning, low-rank tensor factorization, zero-cost Neural Architecture Search, edge and embedded inference).

QUANTIZATION · PRUNING · TENSOR FACTORIZATION · ZERO-COST NAS · EDGE INFERENCE
03 AXIS / LATENT SPACES

Geometry and AI

How to leverage the geometry of latent spaces or input domains to better adapt and monitor AI systems (graph signal processing, Graph Neural Networks, manifold learning, Riemannian and hyperbolic representations).

GRAPH SIGNAL PROCESSING · GNN · MANIFOLD LEARNING · RIEMANNIAN & HYPERBOLIC SPACES
SECTION 02 [THEMES]

Transversal Research Themes

METHODS & APPLICATION DOMAINS · 01 TO 08
02.0

Domain Matrix

Across our three core pillars, research activities span eight fundamental and applied domains ranging from brain dynamics and bioacoustics to hardware error-correcting decoders.

T.01 ADAPTATION

Few-shot & transfer

Rapid generalization from limited supervision and foundation model transfer.

T.02 HARDWARE

Efficient & edge AI

Resource-constrained neural networks, compression, and embedded inference.

T.03 TOPOLOGY

Graphs & geometry

Graph signal processing, GNN architectures, and non-Euclidean latent spaces.

T.04 RELIABILITY

Robustness

Out-of-distribution monitoring, generalization guarantees, and resilient representations.

T.05 NEURO

Neuroscience & EEG

Foundation models for neuroimaging, brain-computer interfaces, cortical dynamics.

T.06 ACOUSTICS

Audio & music

Structural music analysis, bioacoustics, auditory representations.

T.07 COMMS

Channel coding

Polar codes, error-correcting decoders, hardware transceivers.

T.08 SOLVERS

Optimization

Combinatorial optimization, metaheuristics.
