Human-Scaled & Resource-Conscious AI Research

We study representations that help intelligent systems learn, adapt, and make sense of complex data across signals, images, and brains.

BRAIN is a research team of Lab-STICC (UMR CNRS 6285) hosted at IMT Atlantique in Brest, France, within the Mathematical and Electrical Engineering (MEE) department. We investigate fundamental and applied questions at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, Signal Processing, Computer Vision, Audio, and Computational Neuroscience.

Core Scientific Directions

Three Foundational Pillars

Rather than relying on unconstrained scaling, our research prioritizes parsimonious learning, mathematical structure, and energy-conscious representations.

Pillar 01

Thrifty AI

How to find efficient representations in the low-data regime (few-shot learning, zero-shot adaptation, vision-language models, transfer learning, foundation model adaptation).

Few-shot learning Zero-shot adaptation Vision-Language Foundation models
Pillar 02

Compression of AI

How to find efficient representations to lower the computational and energy demands of AI systems (quantization, pruning, low-rank tensor factorization, zero-cost Neural Architecture Search, edge and embedded inference).

Quantization & Pruning Tensor factorization Zero-cost NAS Edge inference
Pillar 03

Geometry and AI

How to leverage the geometry of latent spaces or input domains to better adapt and monitor AI systems (graph signal processing, Graph Neural Networks, manifold learning, Riemannian and hyperbolic representations).

Graph Signal Processing GNNs Manifold learning Riemannian & Hyperbolic
Transverse Research Matrix

Eight Interconnected Themes

From human neuroimaging and structural musicology to hardware error-correcting transceivers, our methods bridge foundational mathematics and real-world physical signals.

Brain & Signals

Neuroscience & EEG

Foundation models for neuroimaging, brain-computer interfaces, and cortical dynamics across spatial-temporal scales.

Acoustics

Audio & music

Structural music analysis, bioacoustics, and interpretable auditory representations for natural soundscapes.

Frugal Learning

Few-shot & transfer

Rapid adaptation of pretrained vision, audio, and multimodal foundation models under scarce supervision.

Embedded Systems

Efficient & edge AI

Low-rank tensor decomposition, quantization, and zero-cost architecture search for constrained hardware.

Manifolds

Graphs & geometry

Graph signal processing, Graph Neural Networks, and Riemannian or hyperbolic geometry of latent spaces.

Reliability

Robustness

Monitoring latent distributions, out-of-distribution generalization, and trustworthy behavior under domain shift.

Communications

Channel coding

Polar codes, high-throughput error-correcting decoders, and energy-efficient hardware transceivers.

Algorithms

Optimization

Combinatorial optimization, metaheuristics, and proximal solver formulations for structured learning problems.

Recent Contributions

Selected Publications

Representative works illustrating our focus on EEG foundation models, kernel adaptation, tensorized low-rank compression, and hardware-aware architecture search.

Neuroscience & EEG Foundation Models

REVE: A Foundation Model for EEG

Yassine El Ouahidi, Jonathan Lys, Philipp Thölke, Nicolas Farrugia, Bastien Pasdeloup, Vincent Gripon, Karim Jerbi

A 4D spatial-temporal foundation model pretrained on 60,000+ hours of EEG designed to adapt seamlessly to diverse clinical and cognitive tasks.

Few-shot & transfer Vision-Language

ProKeR: A Kernel Perspective on Few-Shot Adaptation of Large Vision-Language Models

Yassir Bendou, Amine Ouasfi, Vincent Gripon, Adnane Boukhayma

Reformulating few-shot adaptation of CLIP-like models through proximal kernel ridge regression in feature space.

Compression of AI Efficient & edge AI

TensLoRA: Tensor Alternatives for Low-Rank Adaptation

Axel Marmoret, Reda Bensaid, Jonathan Lys, Vincent Gripon, François Leduc-Primeau

Leveraging higher-order tensor factorizations across attention projections and layers to achieve superior parameter compression.

Efficient & edge AI NAS

Insights From a Data- and Space-Agnostic Approach to Zero-Cost Proxies

Timotée Ly-Manson, Mathieu Léonardon, Abdeldjalil Aïssa-El-Bey

Disentangling architectural topology from input data in zero-cost Neural Architecture Search proxies to enable fast hardware-aware design.

We welcome collaborations on frugal AI, neural data analysis, audio processing, and embedded architectures.
