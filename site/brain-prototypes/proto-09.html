BRAIN

Broader Artificial Intelligence
Technopôle Brest-Iroise · France

We study representations that help intelligent systems learn, adapt, and make sense of complex data across signals, images, and brains.

BRAIN is a research team of Lab-STICC (UMR CNRS 6285) hosted at IMT Atlantique in Brest, France, within the Mathematical and Electrical Engineering (MEE) department. We investigate fundamental and applied questions at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, Signal Processing, Computer Vision, Audio, and Computational Neuroscience.

Core Research Pillars
1. Thrifty AI PIL-01

How to find efficient representations in the low-data regime (few-shot learning, zero-shot adaptation, vision-language models, transfer learning, foundation model adaptation).

2. Compression of AI PIL-02

How to find efficient representations to lower the computational and energy demands of AI systems (quantization, pruning, low-rank tensor factorization, zero-cost Neural Architecture Search, edge and embedded inference).

3. Geometry and AI PIL-03

How to leverage the geometry of latent spaces or input domains to better adapt and monitor AI systems (graph signal processing, Graph Neural Networks, manifold learning, Riemannian and hyperbolic representations).
