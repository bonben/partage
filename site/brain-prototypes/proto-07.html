We study representations that help intelligent systems learn, adapt, and make sense of complex data across signals, images, and brains.

BRAIN is a research team of Lab-STICC (UMR CNRS 6285) hosted at IMT Atlantique in Brest, France, within the Mathematical and Electrical Engineering (MEE) department. We investigate fundamental and applied questions at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, Signal Processing, Computer Vision, Audio, and Computational Neuroscience.

Selected Publications

RECENT LANDMARK CONTRIBUTIONS
Neuroscience & EEG Foundation Models

REVE: A Foundation Model for EEG

Yassine El Ouahidi, Jonathan Lys, Philipp Tholke, Nicolas Farrugia, Bastien Pasdeloup, Vincent Gripon, Karim Jerbi

A 4D spatial-temporal foundation model pretrained on 60,000+ hours of EEG designed to adapt seamlessly to diverse clinical and cognitive tasks.

Few-shot & transfer Vision-Language

ProKeR: A Kernel Perspective on Few-Shot Adaptation of Large Vision-Language Models

Yassir Bendou, Amine Ouasfi, Vincent Gripon, Adnane Boukhayma

Reformulating few-shot adaptation of CLIP-like models through proximal kernel ridge regression in feature space.

Compression of AI Efficient & edge AI

TensLoRA: Tensor Alternatives for Low-Rank Adaptation

Axel Marmoret, Reda Bensaid, Jonathan Lys, Vincent Gripon, Francois Leduc-Primeau

Leveraging higher-order tensor factorizations across attention projections and layers to achieve superior parameter compression.

Efficient & edge AI NAS

Insights From a Data- and Space-Agnostic Approach to Zero-Cost Proxies

Timotee Ly-Manson, Mathieu Leonardon, Abdeldjalil Aissa-El-Bey

Disentangling architectural topology from input data in zero-cost Neural Architecture Search proxies to enable fast hardware-aware design.
